a note from me

I'm Maheima.

If you're here, chances are something in your life isn't sitting right. Somewhere you feel stuck. A pattern you keep noticing. A weight you've been carrying without quite naming.

I work with people on the parts of themselves that thinking and talking alone haven't been able to reach. Old beliefs. Feelings that have been running quietly. Sometimes the answer is close to the surface. Sometimes it lives further back, in younger versions of us, or in stories that came down through family.

Every person's map is different, and the pace is theirs to set. What surfaces gets space to settle in before we go further. My role is to hold the space and follow what's ready, not to push.

I'm an internationally certified therapist. IIM Bangalore, class of 2005, with 20+ years in senior marketing roles alongside this practice. Based in Bengaluru, working online.

what I offer

Most of my work is 1:1.

It takes different shapes depending on what someone comes with. Alongside private sessions, I currently run two focused programs.

on money

Worth-Gap

A 1:1 program for senior professionals sitting with a quiet, persistent feeling: that they're worth more than they've let themselves ask for.

They've had long careers, and things have gone well. What sits underneath isn't about the market or the numbers. It's about the small moments where they find themselves saying less, quoting lower, or not asking at all. Then walking away wondering why.

Worth-Gap works with what's actually driving those moments. Delivered 1:1, online, as either a 4-week or 12-week engagement depending on where a client is starting from. Fully screened at the start. Measured before and after.

a daily practice

Emotional Fitness

A live, twice-daily group practice for building everyday emotional strength.

Most of us know what it's like to have a day that leaks. A short fuse with people we love. Sleep that doesn't quite restore. A tiredness sitting underneath everything. And most of us were never taught how to work with any of it.

Emotional Fitness treats this the way we treat physical fitness. 15 minutes in the morning to start steady. 15 minutes at night to put the day down. Same instructor, live on Zoom. Small enough to actually stick to.

The first 14 days are free.

Your Money Story

A 3-hour workshop for B-schools and mid-senior professionals. Each of us carries a money story we never wrote, mostly absorbed young from what wasn't said. This is a gentle session for finding yours, seeing where it came from, and taking home a simple tool to keep working with it. The bigger aim: slowly lowering the shame around money conversations in India.
