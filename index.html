a note from me

I'm Maheima.

on money

Worth-Gap

You are worth more than you're being paid. And somewhere inside, you've known it for a while.

Many of the senior professionals I work with share a quiet, persistent feeling: they are worth more than they are receiving. It shows up in small ways. What they are comfortable asking for. The conversation they want to have but can't bring themselves to. The role they don't believe they can apply for.

Worth-Gap is a private, one-to-one program built for exactly this. Together we look underneath these experiences, understand what's driving them, and gently begin to shift how you relate to money and to your own sense of worth.

Delivered online over 4 weeks. It begins with an assessment, then combines deep one-to-one sessions with simple daily practices to shift how you relate to money and help the change settle in. You are held gently throughout, as you come face to face with the quiet beliefs and patterns that have been keeping you where you are. And you take home tools and practices to shift them, so you can move past them for good.

a daily practice

Emotional Fitness

Some days feel like they leak into everything else. You snap at the kids over something small, and the guilt sits with you all evening. A conversation from the afternoon replays in your head at 1 am. You wake up already tired, before the day has even started.

Nobody ever taught us how to handle what we feel: the anger, the exhaustion, the overthinking mind that won't switch off.

But emotional fitness is a muscle that can be built, just like physical fitness.

The program is 15 minutes, twice a day, live on Zoom. A morning session to start the day steady, and an evening one to put it down before sleep. The same small group shows up each time, so you are never doing this alone.

The first 14 days are free.
