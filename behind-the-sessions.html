Behind the Sessions

People often want to understand what actually happens in a session before they begin. That's fair.

The deeper 1:1 work draws on a few established approaches, each suited to a different kind of block. I don't pick them in advance. We start with what you bring, and the method follows what's there.

None of this needs belief to work. You don't have to be convinced of anything. You just have to be willing to show up and notice what shifts.

Hypno-regression therapy is a deep, transformative technique that works by accessing the subconscious mind, the part of you that stores the memories, emotions, and patterns shaping how you think and behave. It's a way of understanding and resolving the root causes of challenges you face, whether they come from childhood, past events, or inherited emotional patterns.

Debunking the myths

You've probably seen hypnosis dramatized in movies, people clucking like chickens or losing control. That isn't what this is. In hypno-regression therapy you stay fully aware and fully in control. Think of it as a calm, focused state, much like daydreaming or being lost in a good book. You're aware of your surroundings, but you can reach the deeper layers of your mind and explore memories and emotions with real clarity.

How it works

The subconscious is where many of our blocks, insecurities, and unhelpful patterns begin. They can show up as fears, limiting beliefs, or unresolved emotions that quietly shape daily life. By gently guiding you into that relaxed, focused state, the work helps you uncover and heal the root cause, rather than managing the surface. The result is lasting relief, more clarity, and a sense of lightness as you release what no longer serves you.

It can help with a wide range of things, including:

  • Anxiety and stress
  • Fears and phobias
  • Relationship issues
  • Low self-esteem
  • Unexplained physical aches and pains
  • Past trauma or unresolved emotions

A session usually begins with a calming process to help you settle. As you enter that peaceful, focused state, your subconscious becomes more accessible. You might revisit earlier experiences, sometimes ones you thought you'd forgotten, and see with fresh eyes how they shaped your present. From there, you're gently guided to release trapped emotions, reframe past events, and rewrite the beliefs that have been holding you back.

Family Constellation Therapy explores how unresolved issues in your family system can quietly shape your emotional wellbeing, your relationships, and your choices. It rests on a simple idea: we're deeply connected to our family histories, and patterns of behavior, trauma, or dysfunction can pass down through generations without anyone meaning them to.

The work brings these hidden dynamics into view, and finds resolution by addressing the root of recurring challenges, whether they show up as emotional struggles, relationship difficulties, or a feeling of being stuck.

Why it's powerful

Picture your family as a web of connections. When one part of the web is under tension, from old trauma, unspoken truths, or unresolved conflict, it ripples across generations. This work helps identify and gently untangle those knots. In doing so, you free yourself from burdens you may not even know you've been carrying, and make room for healthier patterns.

It's effective for:

  • Healing relationship difficulties, romantic, familial, or professional
  • Breaking cycles of self-doubt or guilt
  • Addressing grief, loss, or estrangement within families
  • Unlocking emotional blocks tied to past family trauma

How it works

  • We begin by exploring the challenge or question you're grappling with, like a difficulty in relationships or a pattern that keeps repeating.
  • Using representatives, real participants or symbolic placeholders, we map your family dynamics on a 'field', creating a picture of hidden patterns and tensions.
  • Through observation and gentle guidance, we find the root of the issue and work toward releasing the emotional burden and restoring some harmony in the system.
  • You leave with resolution, clarity, and something to carry forward, often feeling lighter and more grounded.

A few clarifications

It's not about blame.

The work doesn't point fingers. It's about understanding and healing the unseen bonds and loyalties that shape your life, with care.

No prior knowledge required.

You don't need to know detailed family history or share sensitive details for this to work.

It's rooted in family, but the healing is yours.

While it works with family dynamics, the focus is on shifting what you carry and bringing healing to your own life.

Inner Child Work is a gentle way of reconnecting with the younger parts of yourself, especially the ones that felt unseen, unheard, or unsafe. Those "inner children" still live within us, quietly shaping how we think, feel, and respond today.

Whether it's the part that feels rejected in relationships, overwhelmed at work, or scared to speak up, this work helps you meet that part with compassion and care. It isn't about digging up the past endlessly. It's about meeting yourself with the kindness you may not have received back then.

Why it works

We don't simply grow out of childhood. We carry it with us. When we feel triggered, shut down, perfectionistic, needy, avoidant, or chronically anxious, it's often not the adult reacting, but a much younger part still holding old pain or old beliefs.

By slowing down and getting to know that part, rather than shaming it or pushing it away, we begin to loosen the grip of old patterns. The more safety and compassion we offer ourselves, the less power those triggers hold.

A few common myths

"It's just about blaming your parents."

Not at all. This is about understanding, not blaming. It's about acknowledging what you needed then, so you can give it to yourself now.

"I had a good childhood, so this isn't for me."

Even loving families miss emotional cues. This work isn't only for those with trauma. It's for anyone who grew up feeling emotionally misunderstood or unsupported.

"It sounds dramatic, or childish."

It's actually deeply grounding. You're simply honoring the younger parts of you that shaped who you are. This is about emotional maturity, not immaturity.

When to consider it

  • You're tired of holding it all together and want to heal from the inside.
  • You keep attracting similar patterns in relationships.
  • You're doing all the right things and still feel emotionally stuck.
  • You're curious to reconnect with your inner world, safely and with support.
